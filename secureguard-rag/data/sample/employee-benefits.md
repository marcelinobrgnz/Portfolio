# Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance
Full medical coverage starts on **day 1** of employment for employee and dependents.

## Pension
Company contributes **6%** of salary; employee may contribute up to **10%** with matching.

## Learning Budget
Each employee receives **€1,500/year** for courses, conferences, and certifications.

## Wellness
Gym subsidy of **€50/month** and access to the employee assistance program (EAP).

## Annual Leave
**25 days** paid leave per year, plus public holidays and parental leave per HR policy.
