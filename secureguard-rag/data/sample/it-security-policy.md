# IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least **12 characters** and rotated every **90 days**.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is **mandatory** for all employees accessing internal systems and VPN.

## Data Classification
- **Public** — may be shared externally
- **Internal** — employees only
- **Confidential** — need-to-know basis; never email unencrypted

## Incident Reporting
Report suspected security incidents within **1 hour** to **security@company.example**.

## Approved Software
Only software from the company app catalog may be installed on work devices.
