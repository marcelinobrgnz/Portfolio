# SecureGuard RAG — Company Knowledge Base (Sample)

## Parental Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to **26 weeks** of parental leave. At least **2 weeks** must be taken within the first year after birth or adoption.

## Remote Work Policy
Staff may work remotely up to **3 days per week** with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00 IST**.

## Expense Reimbursement
Submit expenses within **30 days** via the finance portal. Receipts are required for amounts over **€25**.

## IT Support
For password resets, contact **it-help@company.example**. For hardware issues, open a ticket in the IT portal. SLA: **4 business hours** for critical issues.

## Code of Conduct
Harassment, discrimination, and sharing confidential data externally are strictly prohibited. Report concerns to **hr@company.example**.
