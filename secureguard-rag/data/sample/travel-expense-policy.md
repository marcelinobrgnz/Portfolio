# Travel & Expense Policy

## Pre-Approval
International travel requires manager approval **at least 14 days** in advance.

## Daily Allowances
- Domestic travel: **€75** per day meals/incidentals
- International travel: **€120** per day meals/incidentals

## Hotel Limits
Hotels must not exceed **€200/night** domestic or **€350/night** international without VP approval.

## Receipts
All expenses over **€25** require itemized receipts uploaded within **30 days**.

## Prohibited Expenses
Personal entertainment, alcohol (unless client dinner), and first-class upgrades without approval.
